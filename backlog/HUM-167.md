Problem: after `up` reports `readiness timed out`, `hum status NAME` still says `readiness: starting` with no diagnostic, indefinitely. Users and agents read that as still booting.

Evidence (2026-11-17, built from 024ec7e): `slow` with `ready: {match: "Listening", timeout: 2s}` never printed `Listening`. `up` exited 2 with `slow: readiness timed out`, and `hum status slow --json` later returned `"readiness":"starting","readiness_diagnostic":null`. internal/app/app.go defines only `starting`, `ready`, and `running_unverified`. README: "every check gives up after `timeout`". design.md: `http`, `tcp`, and `exec` probes stop at the timeout, while a `match` launch keeps recording its first matching cursor even when nobody waits.

Leading direction: when a launch spends its readiness budget without success, record a readiness state for that launch (for example `timed_out`) with a diagnostic, shown in aggregate and named status, CLI JSON v1, and MCP. A relaunch resets it as today.

Non-goals: no retries after the budget; no health monitoring; no change to `up`/`start` exit codes; no restart on timeout.

Open questions before todo: whether `match` flips from timed out to `ready` if the line appears later or stops matching at the timeout; whether the timeout is daemon-owned for every method today; the enum value name (CLI JSON v1 clients already must tolerate new enum values).
